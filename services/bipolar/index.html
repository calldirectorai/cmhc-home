Services · Bipolar disorder

Therapy for bipolar disorder in New York

Support for recognizing mood shifts, building routines and staying steady.

Request an appointment

Living with mood shifts takes energy. We'll work on recognizing early signs, building steady routines around sleep and stress, and strengthening the support around you.

Therapy works alongside any care you receive from your psychiatrist or doctor, and Chantal can coordinate with them with your permission.

How we'll work

  • Early-warning signs and a personal stability plan
  • Routines that protect your mood
  • Support for family relationships

Sessions of 30 or 53 minutes · secure video · English, French or Mina

Insurance & cost

In network with Aetna, Carelon, Cigna, Curative, Fidelis Care, First Health, Medicare, Optum, Oxford and Tricare East. New York Medicaid is coming soon. Self-pay sessions are $200. Coverage is confirmed with you before your first session.
