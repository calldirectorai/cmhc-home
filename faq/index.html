FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about getting started, insurance, fees and how sessions work.

How do I get started?

Send a request on the request an appointment page with your name and the best time to reach you. Chantal calls you within one business day to answer questions and confirm your insurance or self-pay, and then you meet by secure video.

Are sessions in person or online?

All sessions are online by secure video, for clients anywhere in New York State. You only need a private space and a phone, tablet or computer with a camera.

Who will I see?

Individual therapy for children, teens and adults, and evaluations, are with Chantal Amoussou, LMHC‑D, CASAC, ADHD‑CCSP. Couples counseling is with Emma Collado, MHC‑LP, a Mental Health Counselor with a New York State limited permit who practices under Chantal's clinical supervision.

Which languages do you offer?

Individual sessions are in English, French or Mina with Chantal. Couples counseling is in English or Haitian Creole (Kreyòl) with Emma.

Do you take insurance?

Yes. Chantal is in network with Aetna, Carelon, Cigna, Curative, Fidelis Care, First Health, Medicare, Optum, Oxford and Tricare East. New York Medicaid is coming soon. Your coverage is confirmed before your first session. See insurance & fees.

How much does it cost without insurance?

Self-pay individual sessions and couples sessions are $200 each. Immigration psychosocial evaluations are $1,500 flat, with a rush report for $300 more. If you are uninsured or self-pay, you can ask for a Good Faith Estimate.

When do I pay?

Self-pay sessions are paid before each session. If you use insurance, any copay or coinsurance is based on your plan.

Is couples counseling covered by insurance?

No. Couples counseling is self-pay at $200 per session.

Do you see children and teens?

Yes, from age 6. A parent or guardian requests the appointment and stays involved.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday from 7 am to noon and 4 to 10 pm, and Saturday and Sunday from 7 am to 9 pm, Eastern time, by appointment.

What is the patient portal?

Once you are a client, the secure patient portal is where you complete intake forms, sign documents, see your appointments and invoices, and send secure messages. Chantal sends you an invitation to set it up.

Can my attorney refer me for an immigration evaluation?

Yes. Attorneys can refer clients directly on the immigration evaluations page. Evaluations are offered in English, French or Mina.

What if I am in crisis?

Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 if you are in immediate danger. This practice does not provide emergency services.
