Services

What we can work on together

Individual online therapy, assessments and immigration evaluations for children from age 6, teens and adults, anywhere in New York State, in English, French or Mina.

Immigration evaluations

Asylum, VAWA, U & T Visa and extreme hardship waiver evaluations, for individuals and attorneys.

Trauma & domestic violence

Trauma-informed support for survivors of domestic violence, trauma and PTSD.

Mental health assessments

Diagnostic, depression, anxiety, trauma and substance use assessments, with treatment recommendations.

Anxiety

Worry, racing thoughts and tension that get in the way of daily life.

Depression

Low mood, loss of interest and feeling stuck, with steady support to move forward.

ADHD

Focus, organization and follow-through, for children, teens and adults.

Substance use

Support for changing your relationship with alcohol or drugs, from a CASAC.

Anger management

Understand what sets you off and respond in ways you feel good about.

Children & teens

Therapy for ages 6 to 17, with parents involved along the way.

Bipolar disorder

Tools to understand mood shifts, build routines and stay steady.

OCD

Practical, evidence-based strategies for intrusive thoughts and compulsions.

Stress

Work, family and life transitions, with space to breathe and regroup.

Couples counseling

Communication, conflict and reconnecting, with a clinician on our team. English or Haitian Creole.

How sessions work

  • Individual sessions of 30 or 53 minutes, by secure video
  • Monday to Friday 7 am to noon and 4 to 10 pm, Saturday and Sunday 7 am to 9 pm (Eastern)
  • Clients under 18 with a parent or guardian involved
  • Approaches include CBT, motivational interviewing and trauma-informed care

Not offered

In-person sessions and medication prescribing. If you are in crisis, call or text 988, or call 911 if you are in immediate danger.
