Evaluations for the filings that need them most

Each evaluation is written for the specific filing your attorney is preparing, and for your own story.

Asylum

Documents the psychological impact of persecution, violence or threats experienced before coming to the United States.

VAWA

For survivors of abuse by a U.S. citizen or permanent resident spouse, parent or adult child. Trauma-informed documentation of how the abuse has affected you.

U Visa

For victims of qualifying crimes. Documents the mental and emotional harm that resulted from the crime.

T Visa

For survivors of human trafficking. Documents trauma and its effects, with care and at your own pace.

Extreme hardship waiver

Documents the psychological hardship a U.S. citizen or permanent resident family member would face from separation or relocation.

Not sure which applies?

Your attorney decides the filing. Chantal tailors the evaluation to it and confirms the scope with you, or with your attorney, before scheduling.

How an evaluation works

  1. Intake callChantal confirms the case type, the filing deadline and the fee with you or your attorney.
  2. Clinical interviewOne or more interviews by secure video, with standardized screening measures, in English, French or Mina.
  3. Written reportPsychosocial history, clinical findings, diagnosis where applicable and treatment recommendations, sent to your attorney.

Fees & timing

Immigration evaluations are self-pay, because they are prepared for legal purposes, and are usually not covered by insurance. The fee is confirmed with you before scheduling. Ongoing therapy afterwards can be billed to insurance.

Common questions

Which immigration cases do you write evaluations for?

Asylum, VAWA, U Visa, T Visa and extreme hardship waivers. If your case is different, ask Chantal.

How much does an evaluation cost?

$1,500 flat per evaluation, including the clinical interview, screening and written report. A rush report is $300 more. Evaluations are self-pay.

How long does the report take?

Within 14 days of the final interview. Rush reports are available for tight filing deadlines.

Can the evaluation be done in French?

Yes. Evaluations are available in English, French and Mina.

I'm an immigration attorney. How do I refer a client?

Use the referral form on this page, or call (516) 614-4116. Please share contact details only, not case facts or health information. Chantal will call to discuss scope and timing.

Is the evaluation covered by insurance?

Evaluations for legal purposes are usually not covered by insurance. Ongoing therapy afterwards can be billed to insurance.
