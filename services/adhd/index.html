Services · ADHD

ADHD support for children, teens and adults

Practical strategies from an ADHD‑Certified Clinical Services Provider (ADHD‑CCSP).

Request an appointment

ADHD affects focus, organization, time, emotions and relationships, at home, at school and at work. Chantal holds the ADHD‑Certified Clinical Services Provider credential and builds strategies that fit how your brain works.

For children and teens, parents are part of the plan, with tools for routines, homework and emotional regulation. For adults, we focus on planning, follow-through and the stress ADHD can bring. Chantal does not prescribe medication, and she can work alongside your doctor if you take it.

How we'll work

  • Routines, planning and focus strategies that stick
  • Parent guidance for children and teens
  • Support for frustration, self-esteem and emotional ups and downs

Sessions of 30 or 53 minutes · secure video · English, French or Mina

In network with Aetna, Carelon, Cigna, Curative, Fidelis Care, First Health, Medicare, Optum, Oxford and Tricare East. New York Medicaid is coming soon. Self-pay sessions are $200. Coverage is confirmed with you before your first session.

Common questions

Do you work with children who have ADHD?

Yes, from age 6, with parents or guardians closely involved.

Do you prescribe ADHD medication?

No. Chantal provides therapy and practical strategies, and can coordinate with your or your child's doctor.

When are sessions available?

Monday to Friday from 7 am to noon and 4 to 10 pm, and Saturday and Sunday from 7 am to 9 pm, Eastern time, by secure video. Sessions last 30 or 53 minutes.
